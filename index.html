Seen Geometry

Short WebGL pictures so a student can see what a matrix does. One idea on each page. The picture is the explanation.

  1. 01 Instanced field One box. Many matrices. One draw call.
  2. 02 Morph targets Spike and squash, blended with two weights.
  3. 03 Order of transforms Same translate, rotate, and scale. Three products.

Pages address, after a repository admin enables Pages from the main branch at the root: virgilrenfroe.github.io/seen-geometry. On your computer, run python3 -m http.server 8080 in this folder and open the hub in a browser. The modules do not load from a file:// address.

An educator directed AI agents to make these pages. The author is not a software engineer and does not hold a mathematics appointment. Notes live in the README.
