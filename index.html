Fourteen lessons

SeenGeometry

14 lessons

Fourteen pictures. Each one is a matrix acting on a shape.

  1. 01

    Instanced field

    One box. Many matrices. Every copy is drawn in one pass.

    Games, cities, and repeated parts
  2. 02

    Morph targets

    Spike and squash. Two weights move the same points.

    Faces, speech, and product shapes
  3. 03

    Order of transforms

    Same translate, rotate, and scale. Three products. Three pictures.

    Robots, bones, and assemblies
  4. 04

    Point vs direction

    w = 1 moves. w = 0 stays. Same translation.

    Normals, maps, and physics
  5. 05

    Shear

    The area stays 1. The corner angle changes.

    Type, drawings, and scans
  6. 06

    Determinant and reflection

    A negative determinant flips the shape. Zero flattens it.

    Area, mirrors, and warnings
  7. 07

    Fold

    A crease reflects the sheet. The mark flips.

    Symmetric parts and folded sheets
  8. 08

    Rotation

    A point on the circle. Two folds make one turn.

    Cameras, joints, and sensors
  9. 09

    Projection

    A house drops onto a plane. Top, front, and side. det = 0.

    Plans, elevations, and drawings
  10. 10

    Perspective

    Move the eye. Divide by w. Depth lines meet on the horizon.

    Cameras, drawings, and phones
  11. 11

    Inverse

    Apply a matrix, then its inverse. The product is the identity.

    CAD, cameras, and a teaching cipher
  12. 12

    Eigenvectors

    Most arrows turn. The red ones stay on a line and stretch by λ.

    Search, data, and structures
  13. 13

    Dot product

    Drag two arrows. The dot product is |a||b|cos θ.

    Work, light, and search
  14. 14

    Cross product

    Drag two arrows in the plane. The red arrow is a × b, and its length is the area.

    Normals, torque, and maps
