07

Fold

Drag the crease. One half of the sheet folds over that line. A fold is a reflection.

The line passes through the origin. The determinant is −1. The mark flips.


    
    
    
The red mark turns the crease. The ink mark slides it. A fold always flips handedness, as on the determinant page.

    

      Reflection across the crease.
      
    

    

      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • A mechanical designer often models half of a symmetric part, then mirrors that half to finish it.
    • 
        
  • Pattern designers repeat a motif by reflection. A kaleidoscope does the same thing with mirrors.
    • 
        
  • In optics, a mirror sends a point or a light ray to its reflection across a line or a plane.
    • 
        
  • Engineers study flat folds so a solar panel or a stent can pack into a small space and then open. A flat fold reflects part of the sheet across a crease.
    •
