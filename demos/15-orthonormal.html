Orthonormal basis

Drag either tip. u and v are not perpendicular. Walk the steps. First make a unit arrow along u. Then subtract the shadow of v, and divide what remains by its length. The red arrows are the frame: each has length 1, and their dot product is 0.

These two arrows leave the same origin. The angle between them is not a right angle, so they are not a frame yet.

      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • In CAD and in BIM, a local frame sits on a wall, a floor, or a part. A CAD specialist or a BIM coordinator measures along those axes instead of the axes of the whole building.
    • 
        
  • In 3D graphics and in a game engine, a camera builds its basis from a look-at. A graphics programmer or a game developer takes the direction the camera faces, then builds a right and an up that are unit length and perpendicular.
    • 
        
  • In robotics, the end effector carries its own frame. A robotics engineer reads the gripper's position and orientation from that frame.
    • 
        
  • Related idea. In data science, principal component analysis lines the points up on perpendicular axes of spread. Those axes are eigenvectors. A data scientist uses them to summarize a cloud of points. Lesson 12 shows eigenvectors.
