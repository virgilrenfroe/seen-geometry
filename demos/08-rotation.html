      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • Games turn a camera or a character with a rotation.
    • 
        
  • Many robot joints are rotations. A robotics engineer sets those angles.
    • 
        
  • An aerospace engineer describes the attitude of a satellite or a drone. Attitude is a set of rotations.
    • 
        
  • A phone turns its screen with a rotation. Motion sensors report how the phone has turned.
    • 
        
  • Animation tools store a turn as a rotation of a bone or an object.
