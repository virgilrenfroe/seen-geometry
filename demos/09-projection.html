      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • Architects and architectural drafters draw a plan and elevations. The plan is the top view. An elevation is the front or the side. Each view drops one direction.
    • 
        
  • Mechanical drafters and CAD designers do the same for a part. The front, top, and side views sit together on one sheet.
    • 
        
  • In games, a related idea is a shadow map. A light flattens the scene onto a plane to draw a shadow. That map is not an architectural plan.
    • 
        
  • In cartography, a related idea is a map projection. A cartographer flattens the Earth onto a sheet. Information is lost, but the matrix is not this one.
