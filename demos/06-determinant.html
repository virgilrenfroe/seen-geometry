      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • The absolute value of the determinant tells how much area or volume grows or shrinks. In physics and engineering, the same factor is called the Jacobian.
    • 
        
  • A technical artist checks the sign. A negative determinant means the model was mirrored, and the surface normals can face the wrong way.
    • 
        
  • In chemistry, a related idea is chirality. Some mirror-image molecules are not the same as the original.
    • 
        
  • A determinant of zero is a warning. The shape has fallen onto a line or a plane. A set of equations may then have no single answer.
