Dot product

Drag either tip. The dot product is a·b = |a||b|cos θ. The red measure is the shadow of a on the line of b.

The angle is less than 90°. The dot product is positive. The shadow falls forward along b.

The shadow length is a·b divided by the length of b. That shadow is a projection onto a line. Lesson 09 projects a house onto a plane. A projection drops a direction, and the lost direction does not come back.

    

      
    

    

      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • In physics and in mechanical engineering, work is force · displacement. Only the part of the force along the motion counts. A physicist or a mechanical engineer computes that product.
    • 
        
  • On a matte surface, brightness follows Lambert's cosine law. It is the dot product of the surface normal and the direction toward the light. A lighting artist and a graphics programmer use this.
    • 
        
  • Search and recommendation compare two lists of numbers by the cosine of the angle between them. That cosine is a dot product of two directions. A data scientist or a search engineer uses it to find similar items.
    • 
        
  • In a game, a triangle that faces away from the camera is often skipped. The sign of the face normal · the view direction tells which way the triangle points. A graphics programmer uses that test.
