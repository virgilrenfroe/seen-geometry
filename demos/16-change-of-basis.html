Change of basis

The arrow v is fixed on the page. Slide the angle to turn the red frame. The pair (x, y) is v in the page axes. The pair (x′, y′) is the same arrow in the frame. Each primed coordinate is a shadow: a dot product with one red axis.

The frame has turned 45° from the page axes. The page coordinates stay (3.00, 1.00). In the frame, the coordinates are the shadows of v on e1 and on e2.

      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • In a game engine and in film visual effects, a camera has its own axes and the scene has another. A graphics programmer or a visual effects artist converts a point from world coordinates into the camera frame, and back.
    • 
        
  • In CAD and in mechanical design, a hole is stored in the part's own axes. A CAD specialist or a mechanical designer then places that part into the assembly, which uses another frame.
    • 
        
  • In aerospace and in robotics, the body frame rides with the craft and the inertial frame stays with the ground. An aerospace engineer or a robotics engineer reads the same velocity in both frames.
    • 
        
  • Related idea. In data science, principal component analysis gives a new frame for the points. A data scientist writes each point in those feature coordinates. The axes are eigenvectors. Lesson 12 shows eigenvectors.
