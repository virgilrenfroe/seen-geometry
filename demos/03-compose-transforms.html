Order changes the picture

The three numbers stay the same. The grid stays still. Only the product changes.

Read the product from right to left

The rightmost matrix touches the point first. The dark dot is the origin. The origin does not move with the arrow.

M = T · R · S

Scale first. Then rotate. Then translate. The arrow turns about its tail. Then it steps along X.

      Where this is used
      
    
        
  • A robotics engineer places a robot hand by combining the moves of each joint. The order of those moves changes where the hand goes. This is called forward kinematics.
    • 
        
  • A character rigger stacks bones. Each bone moves in the frame of the bone above it.
    • 
        
  • A 3D tool stores a scene as a tree. A move on a parent carries every child.
    • 
        
  • A mechanical designer places parts in an assembly. A turn and then a slide is not the same as a slide and then a turn.
