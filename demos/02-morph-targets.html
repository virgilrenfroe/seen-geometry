Two blend shapes

The base shape is a sphere. Spike and squash move the same points. A slider sets a weight. Both weights can be on at once.

Weights

At 0 you see the base sphere. At 1 that shape is fully on. The two weights add together. Their sum can be more than 1.

Where this is used
  • Character artists and riggers build face shapes on one mesh. Animators blend those shapes for a smile or for speech. This is how many films and games move a face.
  • The points stay on the same model. They do not jump to a new mesh.
  • A product page can blend two shapes of one object, so a viewer watches the form change.
  • In medicine, a related method blends two shapes of the same bone or organ to show a change.
